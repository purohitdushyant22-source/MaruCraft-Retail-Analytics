Key Business Insights
1. Top Revenue Driver
 
   The Handwoven Cotton Rug generated the highest revenue of ₹504,756.27, making it the company's primary revenue driver. Revenue concentration among a small number of products indicates that overall business performance is highly dependent on these key products.

    Recommendation: Prioritize inventory availability, pricing optimization, and promotional campaigns for top-performing products to prevent stockouts and sustain revenue growth.

2. Most Profitable Category

    Home Decor emerged as the most profitable category, generating ₹624,361.57 in total profit, more than double the profit generated by Textiles (₹289,668.05).

    Recommendation: Expand the Home Decor product portfolio and allocate additional marketing resources to this category while maintaining healthy profit margins.

3. Highest Profit-Generating Product

    The Handwoven Cotton Rug was also the most profitable product, generating ₹255,856.27 in total profit. Strong performance in both revenue and profitability suggests high customer demand combined with healthy margins.

    Recommendation: Monitor demand trends closely and ensure adequate inventory levels throughout the year to maximize profitability.

4. Higher Discounts Reduce Profitability

    Profit margins declined consistently as discount levels increased. Average margins decreased from 56.93% in the 0–5% discount range to 44.18% in the above 20% discount range, representing a decline of 12.75 percentage points.

    Recommendation: Reassess discount strategies above 20% and evaluate whether additional sales volume sufficiently compensates for reduced profitability.

5. High Return Rates on Key Sales Channels

    The Website channel recorded the highest return rate at 6.10%, followed closely by Instagram at 5.98%. Elevated return rates can increase logistics costs, operational overhead, and negatively impact overall profitability.

    Recommendation: Conduct product-level return analysis and review customer feedback to identify root causes and improve customer satisfaction.

6. Seasonal Variations in Business Performance

    Monthly revenue analysis revealed noticeable fluctuations throughout the period. November 2025 recorded the highest monthly revenue at ₹285,025.95, indicating potential seasonal demand patterns.

    Recommendation: Align inventory planning, staffing, and promotional activities with high-demand periods to maximize revenue opportunities.

7. Significant Differences in Channel Profitability

    The WhatsApp channel delivered the highest profit margin at 52.15%, followed closely by the Website at 52.04%. In contrast, Marketplace recorded the lowest profit margin at 50.79%.

    Recommendation: Review pricing strategies, discount policies, and product mix on Marketplace to improve channel profitability while continuing to strengthen high-margin channels.

8. Organic Search is the Most Valuable Acquisition Channel

    Organic Search generated the highest order volume (1,102 orders), highest revenue (₹1,088,988.58), and highest profit (₹563,183.57) among all acquisition channels, making it the most effective customer acquisition source in the dataset.

    Recommendation: Continue investing in SEO and organic visibility initiatives. Although marketing spend data was unavailable, Organic Search demonstrated the strongest contribution to revenue and profitability.
